Privacy Policy

Protecting your digital footprint.

Information We Collect

To provide its features, the Bot automatically collects and stores specific necessary data provided by the Discord API:

  • Server Data: Guild IDs, Channel IDs, and Role IDs required to save your specific server configurations (e.g., welcome channels, mute roles).
  • User Data: User IDs to track economy balances, levels, and command cooldowns.
  • Message Content: We DO NOT log, store, or track general chat messages or Direct Messages. Message content is only temporarily processed in memory when executing a command and is immediately discarded.

How We Use Your Information

The collected data is strictly used for the operational functionality of the bot. This includes executing commands, maintaining leaderboards, enforcing server moderation rules setup by admins, and identifying bugs or errors to improve service stability.

Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal identification information to others. Data is only accessible to the bot's core development team for maintenance purposes. We do not use third-party tracking or advertising services within the bot.

Data Retention and Deletion

Data associated with a server is kept as long as the bot remains in the server. If the bot is kicked or removed, server-specific configuration data is typically purged from our active databases within 30 days. Users may also request immediate manual deletion of their data by joining our Support Server and opening a ticket.
