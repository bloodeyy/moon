Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your data.

Last Updated: July 25, 2026

Your privacy is extremely important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information Moon Bot collects from your Discord server and its users, why we need it, and how we keep it safe.

Information We Collect

To provide its core functionality, Moon Bot collects and processes the following data automatically provided by the Discord API:

1.1. Server Data: Guild IDs, Channel IDs, and Role IDs. This is required to save server-specific settings such as welcome channels, mute roles, and logging configurations.

1.2. User Data: User IDs and basic profile information (like usernames/avatars) when commands are executed. This is used for economy balances, moderation tracking, and generating userinfo cards.

1.3. No Message Logging: We DO NOT log, record, or store general chat messages or Direct Messages (DMs). Message content is only processed temporarily in memory to check for command triggers or bad words (if anti-swear is enabled) and is immediately discarded.

How We Use Your Data

2.1. Providing Features: We use your data to execute commands, apply moderation actions, and manage server-specific configurations.

2.2. Service Improvement: We may use aggregated, anonymous data (like command usage statistics) to identify bugs and improve the bot's performance.

2.3. Anti-Abuse: We monitor command execution rates to prevent spam, API abuse, and to maintain the stability of our servers.

Data Sharing and Security

3.1. Zero Selling Policy: We never sell, rent, or trade your personal or server data to any third-party companies, advertisers, or external entities.

3.2. Data Protection: All data is securely stored in our databases using industry-standard encryption protocols. Access to this database is strictly limited to the core development team for maintenance purposes only.

3.3. Discord Integration: As a Discord Bot, our data practices are also bound by Discord's official Privacy Policy.

Data Retention and Deletion

4.1. Server Removal: If Moon Bot is kicked or removed from a server, we typically retain the server's configuration data for 30 days in case of accidental removal. After this period, the data may be permanently purged.

4.2. Right to be Forgotten: Server owners or individual users have the right to request immediate deletion of their data. To do this, please join our Support Server and open a data deletion ticket.

Have privacy concerns?

If you have any questions about how we handle your data, we are here to help.
